- **Official Name (EN):** Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI), Green Energy and Environment Research Laboratories (GEL)
- **Official Name (ZH):** 工業技術研究院 綠能與環境研究所

- **Website:** https://www.itri.org.tw / https://www.itri.org.tw/ListStyle.aspx?DisplayStyle=18&SiteID=1&MmmID=1036425312633575721
- **Research Focus:**

- **Official Name (EN):** National Atomic Research Institute (NARI)

### B2. 加氫站詳細資訊 (Station Details)

#### 楠梓加氫站 (CPC Nanzih Hydrogen Refueling Station)
- **Operator:** 台灣中油股份有限公司 (CPC Corporation, Taiwan)
- **Location:** Nanzih District, Kaohsiung City (高雄市楠梓區)
  - Located at/adjacent to an existing CPC gas station
- **Opened:** December 11, 2025
- **Features:**
  - Taiwan's **first commercial hydrogen refueling station**
  - Dual pressure: 700 bar (light-duty vehicles) + 350 bar (buses/trucks)
  - Part of CPC's "hydrogen energy transportation new chapter"
  - Safety tested and certified by TÜV SÜD (completed Dec 2025 — first HRS safety certification in Taiwan)
- **Cost:** Reportedly ~3x more expensive than a conventional gas station
- **Strategic significance:** Located in Kaohsiung, Taiwan's industrial heartland and hydrogen cluster

#### 樹谷示範性加氫站 (Linde LienHwa Shugu Demo Hydrogen Refueling Station)
- **Operator:** 聯華林德 (Linde LienHwa Co., Ltd.)
- **Location:** Shugu Park (樹谷園區), Xinshi District, Tainan City (台南市新市區)
  - Southern Taiwan Science Park area
- **Opened:** December 11, 2025 (simultaneous with CPC Nanzih)
- **Features:**
  - **Modular design** — scalable, replicable for future Taiwan stations
  - Dual pressure: 700 bar + 350 bar
  - Built to meet global market trends and Taiwan's specific needs
  - Safety certified by TÜV SÜD
- **Strategic significance:**
  - First international-standard HRS in Taiwan (Linde global expertise)
  - Near Southern Taiwan Science Park → potential FCEV fleet applications

### B3. 補助與政策 (Subsidies & Policy)
- **NT$15 million subsidies** provided by Taiwan government for hydrogen refueling stations (Dec 2025)
- Early stage: only ~100 vehicles can use these stations initially
- **Taiwan-Poland Hydrogen Working Group** — 2nd meeting convened Dec 2025 to deepen bilateral cooperation
- "台灣中南部氫能聚落" (Central-Southern Taiwan Hydrogen Cluster) concept under development (May 2026)
- Hydrogen bus pilot: Taiwan hydrogen buses sent to Europe for testing (Dec 2025)

### B4. 未來規劃 (Future Plans)
- Additional stations planned along Taiwan's western corridor
- Focus on heavy-duty transport decarbonization (buses, trucks)
- Hydrogen bus insurance rate assessment for metropolitan areas (May 2026)
- "加氫站共站安全規範" (HRS co-location safety standards) under development with US hydrogen supply chain cooperation discussions (May 2026)
